## 🚀 About Me

I'm a **Full Stack Developer** and **ML Engineer** with a passion for building scalable backend systems and intelligent applications. I love crafting production-grade software — from high-performance APIs and microservices to real-time systems and AI-powered pipelines.

My focus is on writing clean, efficient code, designing robust architectures, and shipping things that actually work at scale. I enjoy the intersection of systems engineering and machine learning — building not just models, but the infrastructure around them.

Currently building **MedConnect**, a full-stack telemedicine platform with real-time video, multi-tenancy, background job processing, and full observability — from scratch.
